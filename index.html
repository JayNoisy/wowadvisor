Your builds

    How builds are chosen

    Every 12 hours we collect builds from top-ranked players and curated guides: Murlok, Peavers, Archon, Wowhead, Method and Icy Veins.

    Each talent is weighted by how often it appears for that spec and content type, and the loadout with the strongest overall agreement wins. The confidence ring shows how much the sources agree.

    Copy a build and we remember it. If the recommended loadout changes later, you'll see a notice here. Sign in to keep that history across devices.
